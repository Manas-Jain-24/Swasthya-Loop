SLIDE 2: PROBLEM STATEMENT & VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHICH HEALTHCARE PROBLEM ARE WE SOLVING?
The breakdown in chronic disease follow-up for patients managing hypertension and type 2 diabetes between quarterly clinic visits.

WHO EXPERIENCES THIS & HOW SERIOUS IS IT?
• Chronic patients are sent home with care plans but lack guidance on daily numbers, leading to missed readings and non-adherence.
• Hospital care coordinators are buried under disconnected records with no way to prioritize which patients need urgent outreach.
• Unmonitored chronic conditions silently escalate into heart attacks, strokes, and kidney failure.

CREDIBLE EVIDENCE & STATISTICS:
• WHO & CDC: Over 50% of chronic disease patients fail to adhere to home treatment and monitoring routines.
• Economic Burden: Chronic conditions drive over 75% of total healthcare spending, largely due to preventable emergency hospitalizations.
• BMJ Quality & Safety: Timely post-discharge follow-up and task verification reduces 30-day hospital readmissions by up to 25%.

LIMITATIONS OF CURRENT SOLUTIONS:
• Paper Logs & Manual Phone Calls: Impossible for hospital coordinators to scale across hundreds of outpatients.
• Generic Health Tracking Apps: Siloed from the hospital, complex to use for elderly patients, and lack clinical accountability.
• Unconstrained "AI Doctor" Chatbots: Hallucinate unvetted medical advice, creating unacceptable legal and clinical liability.

SLIDE 3: PROPOSED SOLUTION & INNOVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OUR PROPOSED SOLUTION:
SwasthyaLoop is a closed-loop platform that connects patients and hospital care coordinators through a safe, auditable Hybrid AI architecture.

KEY FEATURES BY USER:
• For Patients: Clean, simple dashboard to log blood pressure & glucose with instant validation, track daily care tasks, view trend charts, and read plain-language progress summaries.
• For Care Coordinators: Real-time triage board flagging patients with overdue tasks or anomalous trends, complete with audited rule IDs and 1-click resolution.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT & INNOVATIVE?
1. Hybrid AI Safety: We do not use black-box AI for medical decisions. Follow-up flags are triggered by deterministic, transparent code rules (e.g., rule-missing-reading-v1).
2. Constrained Plain-Language AI: Generative AI (Google Gemini) is strictly limited to translating approved clinical facts into reassuring, jargon-free English.
3. Zero Medical Hallucinations: The AI is hard-coded to never diagnose, prescribe, or adjust medication dosages.
4. Resilient Offline Fallback: The platform automatically falls back to deterministic templates if offline, guaranteeing 100% operational uptime.

TECHNOLOGY STACK:
• Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, Lucide Icons.
• Backend: Python 3.13, FastAPI (asynchronous REST API), Pydantic v2 (physiological bounds checking).
• Database: SQLite with SQLAlchemy ORM.
• AI Model: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (via REST API) + Deterministic Rule Engine.
• Testing: Automated test suite using pytest and HTTPX.

SLIDE 4: TECHNICAL METHODOLOGY (WORKFLOW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW: INPUT → PROCESSING → OUTPUT

1. INPUT LAYER:
   • Patient enters vitals (Blood Pressure: Systolic/Diastolic in mmHg; Blood Glucose: mg/dL with context: Fasting/Post-meal).
   • Patient checks off completed daily care tasks (e.g., sodium check, brisk walk).

2. PROCESSING & VALIDATION LAYER:
   • Schema & Physiological Boundary Validation: Pydantic rejects impossible values (e.g., Systolic <= Diastolic or readings outside human limits: 40-300 mmHg).
   • SQLite Database Persistence: Records reading with ISO timestamps and patient ID.
   • Deterministic Rules Engine: Evaluates task deadlines. If a planned reading is overdue (e.g., Patient B's post-lunch glucose), it triggers a traceable flag with source rule ID: `rule-missing-reading-v1`.
   • Fact Aggregator & Constrained AI: Collects structured facts (reading counts, latest values, pending tasks, appointment date) and requests Gemini 1.5 Flash to summarize in plain language. If offline, the built-in deterministic fallback generates the summary instantly.

3. OUTPUT LAYER:
   • Patient Portal: Real-time interactive trend graph, updated task progress, and friendly plain-language summary.
   • Coordinator Portal: Triage dashboard sorted by urgency, displaying exact reason codes and 1-click "Mark Resolved" action.
