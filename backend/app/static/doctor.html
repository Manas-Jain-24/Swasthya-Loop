SwasthyaLoop Clinical Protocol & Eligibility Framework

ICMR 2024 & ACC/AHA Compliant 30-Day Closed-Loop Post-Discharge Architecture

Why post-discharge monitoring? (Jury 1 Defense): Under ICMR and ACC/AHA guidelines, initiating or escalating Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) requires active titration in weeks 1 to 4. Monitoring is not indefinite: each cohort has an explicit exit milestone where monitoring stops.
Cohort A Days 1 - 30 Post-Discharge

30-Day Post-Discharge Cardiac Follow-Up

Clinical Problem: Post-ACS stent discharge on dual-antiplatelet + Telmisartan + Beta-blocker. Preventing hypotensive syncope, bradycardia, or rebound surge.

🎯 Exit Milestone: Day 30 OPD review with stable BP < 130/80 mmHg. Monitoring terminates.
Cohort B Weeks 1 - 4 (28 Days)

Renal & SGLT2i Regimen Watch

Clinical Problem: Diabetic Kidney Disease (CKD Stage 3, eGFR 42) initiating Dapagliflozin + ARB. Guarding against acute eGFR drop > 30% or hyperkalemia.

🎯 Exit Milestone: Day 28 biochemistry confirmation of stable creatinine/eGFR. Monitoring terminates.
Cohort C Week 24 to Postpartum Wk 2

High-Risk Gestational Pre-Eclampsia Watch

Clinical Problem: Pregnant women with gestational hypertension. Early detection of acute pre-eclamptic surges (≥ 140/90) to prevent eclampsia and maternal mortality.

🎯 Exit Milestone: Postpartum Week 2 normotension check. Monitoring terminates.
Cohort D Days 1 - 14 Post-Discharge

Post-Crisis Insulin Stabilization

Clinical Problem: Discharge after severe hyperglycemic crisis on new Basal-Bolus insulin. Guarding against life-threatening nocturnal hypoglycemia (< 70 mg/dL).

🎯 Exit Milestone: Day 14 Glycemic stability (Fasting 100-140 mg/dL, 0 hypo). Monitoring terminates.
Who We Decline to Enroll (Jury 2 Defense): SwasthyaLoop is NOT a wellness app. We intentionally reject over 70% of outpatients to eliminate alert fatigue and ensure high-yield closed-loop coordination.
×
Stable Chronic Disease (> 6 Months) — DECLINED

Patients on unchanged medication with stable vitals require routine semi-annual OPD visits. Remote monitoring induces health anxiety and wastes clinical bandwidth.

→ Action: Referred to routine 6-month primary health centre OPD.
×
Pre-Hypertension without End-Organ Risk — DECLINED

Isolated pre-hypertension (120-139 / 80-89) in non-diabetics is managed exclusively via dietary salt reduction and exercise. Zero clinical yield for remote monitoring.

→ Action: Provided ICMR lifestyle brochure & annual screening.
×
Severe Cognitive Impairment without Proxy Caregiver — DECLINED

Patients unable to self-report vitals without a designated primary caregiver or ASHA worker cannot reliably participate, generating spurious telemetry.

→ Action: Direct social work & community assisted living referral.
×
End-Stage Palliative Care — DECLINED

Comfort-care patients without active curative monitoring goals do not benefit from intensive physiological surveillance.

→ Action: Home palliative care coordination.
India Telemetry Reality (18% Cuff Adoption - Jury 2 Defense): We do NOT rely on consumer smartwatches (which cannot clinically measure BP or glucose) or expensive Bluetooth devices. We support 3 grassroots telemetry pathways.
Tier 1: Community
ASHA & Ayushman Arogya Mandir

Zero hardware cost to patient. Uses existing NHM government NCD kits at village sub-centres. ASHA worker logs readings in 1 tap.

Tier 2: Semi-Urban
Multimodal LCD Photo-OCR

Reads any ₹800 non-smart monitor (Omron, Dr. Morepen, Accu-Chek). Caregiver snaps photo; Gemini extracts SBP/DBP/Glucose with zero typing.

Tier 3: Caregiver
Bilingual Voice & IVR Sync

Daily bilingual Hindi/English voice briefings and WhatsApp/IVR reminders for family members, removing digital literacy barriers.
