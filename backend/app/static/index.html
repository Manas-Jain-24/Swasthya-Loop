Clinical Protocols & Safety Architecture

How SwasthyaLoop prevents medical hallucinations and enforces guideline-backed triage

Layer 1: Deterministic Rules Engine

All clinical flags and severity escalations are calculated purely through deterministic rule definitions. The LLM never makes triage decisions.

  • ICMR 2023 Guidelines: Systolic ≥ 140 or Diastolic ≥ 90 mmHg flagged for routine review.
  • Stage 2 Crisis Rule: Systolic > 160 mmHg escalates immediately to URGENT doctor review.
  • Task Audit: Overdue readings automatically flag patients; completing the vital automatically resolves the flag.
Layer 2: Constrained Gemini LLM

Google Gemini 3.8 Flash is strictly used to translate validated clinical facts into empathetic, patient-friendly plain language.

  • Strict Fact Prompting: The model is given only 4-6 explicit patient facts (BP, glucose, appointment date, completed tasks).
  • Prohibited Term Filter: Output containing words like prescribe, dosage, cure, diagnosis is automatically suppressed.
  • Deterministic Fallback: If network or safety threshold fails, the app falls back to a template summary without crashing.
