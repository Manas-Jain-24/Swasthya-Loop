Defined Clinical Scope
✓ Enroll

Cardiac-discharge patients with a changed BP medicine, for 30 days.

📈 Monitor

Verified BP trajectory and recorded symptom stability check-ins.

🎯 Exit

Stable trajectory or scheduled clinician review at Day 30.

⛔ Do Not Enroll

Emergency symptoms, stable patients without treatment changes, or patients unable to obtain supported measurements.

Doctor Side

Clinician Command Center

For physicians, cardiologists, and care coordinators monitoring post-discharge patients.

  • Hero BP Trend Detection (BP_TREND_UP_3): Flags verified 3-reading upward BP escalation (138/86 → 148/90 → 162/96).
  • AI Clinical Handoff: Concise 2-3 sentence handoff derived strictly from verified database records.
  • Validation Supervisor: Enforces zero drug prescribing, zero dosage changes, and 100% matched claims.
  • Multi-Source Telemetry: Ingests verified readings from device photo, Bluetooth cuff, ASHA, or pharmacy.
  • Action-Oriented Triage: 1-click repeat reading request, outreach documentation, and clinician escalation.
