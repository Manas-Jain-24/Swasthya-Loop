Why Post-Discharge Monitoring? (Jury 1 Counter)

The doctor hasn't failed — patients are in an acute titration window. Under ICMR & ACC/AHA guidelines, initiating GDMT (beta-blockers, SGLT2i, ARBs) mandates 30-day monitoring to prevent readmission or acute renal failure.

4 Niches & Declination Protocol (Jury 2 Counter)

SwasthyaLoop is NOT a wellness app. We intentionally decline over 70% of stable outpatients and focus exclusively on 4 high-stakes niches: 30-day post-PCI, renal titration, gestational pre-eclampsia, and insulin stabilization.

2-Tier Supervised AI (Jury 3 Counter)

Raw LLMs cannot be trusted in healthcare. Our Deterministic Medical Rule Engine supervises Gemini 3.8 Flash, intercepting contraindications (Dual RAS Blockade) and verifying patient eGFR before rendering. 0 hallucinations guaranteed.
