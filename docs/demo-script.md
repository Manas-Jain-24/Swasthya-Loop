| Time | Scene | Talking Points & Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:30** | **Patient Home (Patient A)** | *"Judges, over 70% of chronic care complications happen between doctor appointments because follow-up data is lost. Here is Demo Patient A, managing hypertension. She sees today's monitoring tasks, her latest reading of 132/82 mmHg, her upcoming cardiology appointment, and her longitudinal 5-day blood pressure chart."* |
| **0:30 – 1:00** | **Live Reading Ingestion** | Click **"+ Log Reading"**.<br>Enter systolic: `128`, diastolic: `80`.<br>Click **"Save & Evaluate"**.<br>*"Notice how the reading is validated against physiological rules, the chart immediately reflects the new data point, and the trend indicator updates in real-time."* |
| **1:00 – 1:40** | **The Missing Task (Patient B)** | Switch to **"Patient B (Diabetes)"**.<br>*"Now meet Patient B. Notice the amber alert: a post-lunch glucose reading was scheduled for 1:30 PM but was never logged. Instead of assuming non-adherence or generating an unvetted clinical emergency alert, SwasthyaLoop creates a routine, traceable follow-up flag."* |
| **1:40 – 2:10** | **Explainable AI Summary** | Scroll to the **Plain-Language Summary**.<br>*"Our Hybrid AI engine translates raw numbers into understandable, reassuring language. Importantly, it is strictly grounded in the verified facts shown below, with an automatic deterministic fallback if the AI key is offline. It never hallucinates diagnoses or dosage tweaks."* |
| **2:10 – 3:00** | **Care Coordinator Loop** | Switch to **"Coordinator Triage"**.<br>*"Finally, this is the hospital care-coordinator view. Patient B's overdue task is prioritized at the top with reason code `READING_TASK_OVERDUE` and source rule `rule-missing-reading-v1`. The coordinator contacts the patient, helps them resolve the issue, and clicks **Mark Resolved**. The loop is closed without physician burnout!"* |
